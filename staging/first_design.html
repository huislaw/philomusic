Our Music & Fine Art Programmes

🎹

Classical Piano Lessons

Comprehensive piano instruction from early fundamentals to graded exam preparation (ABRSM / Trinity). Suitable for ages 5 to adults.

🎻

Violin & Cello Tuition

Structured string technique, posture development, and tone production taught by experienced string instructors in private sessions.

🎸

Guitar & Ukulele

Acoustic and classical guitar courses tailored for young learners building finger dexterity, chord mastery, and musical enjoyment.

🎵

Early Childhood Music

Specifically designed musical immersion classes for young toddlers (Ages 2.5 to 5 years) to build rhythm, auditory memory, and pitch awareness.

🎨

Sensory Art Programme

Led by experienced instructor Mr. Tree, tailored for children aged 3 to 5 years old to stimulate creativity and fine motor development.

🖌️

Comprehensive Art Programme

Guided fine art curriculum for students aged 6 and above, covering drawing, sketching, color mixing, and visual expression.

🏛️

Performance & Recitals

Regular performance opportunities and concert stages (e.g., PJPAC) to foster student confidence, stage presence, and ensemble skills.
