Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Toddlers aged 2.5 to 5 can start in our Early Childhood Music or Sensory Art programmes. For formal classical piano, violin, or guitar, we recommend starting around 5 to 6 years old when finger dexterity and focus are suitable.
No prior instrument purchase is required. Our academy provides high-quality acoustic pianos, violins, cellos, guitars, and art media for all introductory assessment sessions.
We specialize in preparing candidates for internationally recognized examination boards including ABRSM (Associated Board of the Royal Schools of Music) and Trinity Guildhall London for both practical and music theory credentials.
Classical instrument lessons (Piano, Violin, Cello, Guitar) are conducted on a private 1-on-1 basis to ensure uncompromised personal guidance. Early Childhood Music and Art classes are run in intimate small groups.
